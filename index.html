Hi, I’m Johir Suresh — a race car driver pursuing a Ph.D. in autonomous vehicles, where I specialize in controls, estimation, and vehicle dynamics. I spend my weekdays teaching machines how to understand motion and make better decisions; on weekends, I do the same thing for myself — usually at a racetrack, in the mountains, through a forest, or on a wonderfully curvy road. When I’m not driving, I’m probably cooking something ambitious or taking far too many pictures.
